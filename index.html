About Kerwin

Welcome to my digital space. I'm a 60-year-old technologist, lifelong learner, and avid fan of heavy riffs. My journey has taken me from the early days of dial-up internet and clunky HTML to the forefront of artificial intelligence, all while maintaining a deep appreciation for the cathartic power of Nu Metal rock.

I believe in the balance of life—much like the Yin and Yang. The chaos of a distorted guitar solo perfectly complements the structured logic of machine learning algorithms. This site is a reflection of my passions, my memories, and my ongoing journey through the digital age.

When I'm not experimenting with new AI models, coding, or tweaking my home server, you'll find me digging through vinyl records, attending live gigs, or mentoring the next generation of tech enthusiasts. Life is about finding the harmony in the noise.

What Life Has Meant for Me

"At sixty, life isn't about winding down; it's about synthesizing everything you've learned into something meaningful. The urgency of youth has been replaced by the clarity of experience."

I've watched technology evolve from room-sized computers to AI that can write poetry, generate art, and compose music. Yet, the human element remains irreplaceable. The angst and energy of Nu Metal taught me that it's okay to feel deeply, to be angry, and to challenge the status quo. Technology taught me that the future is malleable and built by those willing to learn.

Today, my focus is on balance: staying physically active, keeping my mind sharp with new coding challenges, supporting my community, and always leaving room for a loud, unapologetic guitar riff. Life at 60 is not an ending; it's a masterclass in appreciation. It's about knowing who you are, what you love, and having the courage to pursue it unapologetically.
