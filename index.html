About the Keeper

⸸ ⸸ ⸸
🧙 The Keeper

Who Lives Here?

A 60-year veteran of this world, weathered by decades of rock anthems, late-night coding sessions, and conversations that stretched into the small hours. I've watched technology evolve from punch cards to artificial intelligence — and I've loved every cursed moment.

Rock music is my religion. From the howling blues of Sabbath to the cathedral walls of Pink Floyd, from the raw fury of Motörhead to the gothic melancholy of Sisters of Mercy — this music built me, brick by dark brick.

Tech is my second obsession. Hardware, software, the beautiful logic of systems talking to systems. There's poetry in elegant code, and I'll happily debate that with you over a long chat any day.

Obsessions & Pursuits

⸸ ⸸ ⸸
🎵

Rock & Metal

From classic rock to gothic doom, the riff is sacred. Sabbath, Dio, Sisters of Mercy, Fields of the Nephilim — the soundtrack of a life well-lived in beautiful noise.

⚙️

Technology

Hardware hacking, Linux tinkering, networking, AI, and the elegant art of the command line. Tech isn't just a tool — it's a craft worth mastering obsessively.

💬

Good Conversations

Nothing beats a deep, honest conversation — philosophy, music, tech, politics, life, the universe. Pull up a chair and bring your best ideas. All perspectives welcome.

🌐

The Open Web

IRC, forums, old-school internet culture, open source software. The web was built by communities and that spirit still matters. The old ways had wisdom.

📻

Vinyl & Audio

There's no substitute for analogue warmth. A needle in a groove, a proper amp, speakers that move air — music you can feel in your chest the way it was meant to be.

🔭

Curious About Everything

Science, history, the cosmos, human nature, philosophy of mind. Sixty years in and the world still has infinite capacity to fascinate. Long may that last.
