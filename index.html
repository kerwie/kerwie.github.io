My Thoughts on [Current Tech Trend]

The current tech landscape is defined by a pivotal shift from generative AI tools to autonomous agents that execute complex workflows, moving beyond simple content creation into active decision-making. This transition is coupled with an evolution in hardware toward spatial computing, where digital layers overlay physical reality rather than replacing screens. However, the rapid expansion of these technologies faces a critical bottleneck: energy consumption. As data centers and model inference demand unprecedented power, tech giants are becoming energy utilities, making grid capacity and sustainable power sources the next major constraint on growth alongside silicon innovation.

Beyond infrastructure, societal and structural shifts are reshaping value and labor. As AI commoditizes technical skills, the "human premium" rises for roles requiring empathy, curation, and strategic judgment, while a counter-movement of digital minimalism fuels demand for analog experiences. Simultaneously, the convergence of AI with biotechnology promises to accelerate drug discovery and longevity science, though this progress is complicated by geopolitical tensions over semiconductor supply chains. Ultimately, the industry is entering an infrastructure phase where success depends less on software hype and more on solving physical constraints like energy efficiency, biological integration, and regional data sovereignty.
