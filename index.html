About Kerwin

What Life Has Meant For Me

Life has been a profound journey of contrasts and harmonies. From the early days of dial-up internet to the dawn of generative AI, I've watched technology evolve from a mere novelty into a fundamental extension of human creativity. Similarly, the raw, emotive power of Nu Metal taught me that there is profound beauty in chaos and catharsis. Today, my life is about bridging these worlds: embracing the logical precision of code while never losing the soulful, rebellious spirit of a good guitar riff and an honest conversation.
