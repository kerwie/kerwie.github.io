About Kerwin

Hello, I'm Kerwin

A 60-year-old creative soul who believes the best years are the ones where you're still learning, still rocking, and still building. Life has taught me that passion doesn't have an expiration date — it only gets louder.

When I'm not headbanging to Nu Metal riffs, you'll find me tinkering with AI to craft websites, exploring the endless universe of Linux distributions, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible with open-source tools.

Every line of code, every riff, every distro I try — it's all part of the same journey. Love what you do. Stay strong when it breaks. Be resilient enough to try again.
